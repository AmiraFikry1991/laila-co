How this demo works
  1. The AI answers from Nakhla Home's knowledge base and can look up orders (try order 4521, 4522, 4530 or 4588).
  2. Damaged items, big refunds, angry customers or a request for a person go straight to a human in the Agent inbox.
  3. Every conversation is pre-scored for quality and counted in QA and reports.
  4. Agents see who they are talking to: location, local time and device, detected from the connection.
